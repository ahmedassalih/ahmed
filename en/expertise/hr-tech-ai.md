<div class="measure">
  <p>I spent my first years at Royal Air Maroc on the build side: implementing HR Access for
  the group and around ten subsidiaries, working with roughly five developers and twenty HR
  specialists on modules, interfaces and data.</p>
  <p>A few years later I was running an HR department, and I was the one depending on those
  systems to work. Today I look at HR technology from both sides, which is mostly what I am
  useful for.</p>
</div>

<div class="callout">
  <p class="callout__label">From code to boardroom</p>
  <p>I am not a full-time engineer today and I do not present myself as one. What that early
  period gave me is harder to pick up later: I can sit in an executive committee and know what
  a technical answer will cost in practice, and sit with an architect and know what the
  business answer implies for them.</p>
</div>

<h2>The questions I keep coming back to</h2>

<div class="measure">
  <p>Most of my work on HR technology and AI comes down to a small number of practical
  questions. They sound simple. They are usually the ones that have not been answered.</p>
  <ul>
    <li>What problem are we actually solving, and for whom?</li>
    <li>Is the data good enough to support it, and who owns that data?</li>
    <li>How does this connect to the systems already in place?</li>
    <li>What changes for the person who has to use it on a Monday morning?</li>
    <li>Who owns the decision the system is helping to make?</li>
    <li>What happens when it gets something wrong?</li>
  </ul>
</div>

<h2>Where I work</h2>

<div class="deflist">
  <div class="deflist__row">
    <p class="deflist__term">HRIS architecture</p>
    <div class="deflist__desc"><p>Core HR and the tools around it, and what belongs in the
    system of record. Settling this late is what makes integration expensive.</p></div>
  </div>
  <div class="deflist__row">
    <p class="deflist__term">Data and integration</p>
    <div class="deflist__desc"><p>Data models, master data ownership, flows between systems,
    and the reconciliation rules nobody wants to write down.</p></div>
  </div>
  <div class="deflist__row">
    <p class="deflist__term">AI and automation</p>
    <div class="deflist__desc"><p>Where automation removes work, where it simply moves the work
    somewhere less visible, and how to tell the difference before signing.</p></div>
  </div>
  <div class="deflist__row">
    <p class="deflist__term">Generative AI and agents</p>
    <div class="deflist__desc"><p>What these approaches change in an HR process, what they need
    from the data, and which controls have to exist first.</p></div>
  </div>
  <div class="deflist__row">
    <p class="deflist__term">People analytics</p>
    <div class="deflist__desc"><p>Measures an executive committee can act on, built on
    definitions that hold up when finance asks where the number came from.</p></div>
  </div>
  <div class="deflist__row">
    <p class="deflist__term">AI governance</p>
    <div class="deflist__desc"><p>Who decides, what gets reviewed by a person, what is
    explainable, and where the line sits between helping a decision and making it.</p></div>
  </div>
  <div class="deflist__row">
    <p class="deflist__term">Process design</p>
    <div class="deflist__desc"><p>Simplifying a process before automating it. Automating a bad
    process tends to make it permanent.</p></div>
  </div>
  <div class="deflist__row">
    <p class="deflist__term">Adoption</p>
    <div class="deflist__desc"><p>Skills, roles and measurement. This is usually what decides
    whether any of the above produces value.</p></div>
  </div>
</div>

<h2>What it changes in practice</h2>

<div class="measure">
  <ul>
    <li>I assess vendor demonstrations on the architecture behind them rather than the
    interface in front of them.</li>
    <li>I treat data work as a prerequisite, not as a parallel workstream that gets compressed
    at the end.</li>
    <li>I sequence AI ambitions against the actual state of the data, so a pilot that works can
    be scaled.</li>
    <li>I design governance alongside the architecture rather than after the first incident.</li>
  </ul>
</div>
