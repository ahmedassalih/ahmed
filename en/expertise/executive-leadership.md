<h2>What the role actually involves</h2>

<div class="deflist">
  <div class="deflist__row">
    <p class="deflist__term">Business Unit creation</p>
    <div class="deflist__desc"><p>Offer strategy, positioning, pricing logic, delivery model and
    the internal case required to fund all of it.</p></div>
  </div>
  <div class="deflist__row">
    <p class="deflist__term">P&amp;L discipline</p>
    <div class="deflist__desc"><p>Owning revenue and operating performance together. Deciding
    which opportunities to decline is part of the discipline.</p></div>
  </div>
  <div class="deflist__row">
    <p class="deflist__term">Go-to-market strategy</p>
    <div class="deflist__desc"><p>Translating a broad HR transformation proposition into
    something a specific market, in a specific country, will actually buy.</p></div>
  </div>
  <div class="deflist__row">
    <p class="deflist__term">Enterprise business development</p>
    <div class="deflist__desc"><p>Long, complex, multi-stakeholder cycles where the buying
    decision is shared between HR, IT, finance and the executive committee.</p></div>
  </div>
  <div class="deflist__row">
    <p class="deflist__term">Executive relationships</p>
    <div class="deflist__desc"><p>Working with C-level sponsors over the life of a programme —
    including the part after the contract is signed.</p></div>
  </div>
  <div class="deflist__row">
    <p class="deflist__term">Sales → advisory → delivery</p>
    <div class="deflist__desc"><p>Keeping the promise made in the sales cycle connected to what
    the delivery team can architect and operate. This is where credibility is most easily won or
    lost.</p></div>
  </div>
  <div class="deflist__row">
    <p class="deflist__term">Multicultural teams</p>
    <div class="deflist__desc"><p>Leading consultants, architects and managers across countries,
    languages and working cultures in MEA and Europe.</p></div>
  </div>
  <div class="deflist__row">
    <p class="deflist__term">MEA &amp; EMEA leadership</p>
    <div class="deflist__desc"><p>Regional scope with the regulatory, linguistic and
    organisational diversity that comes with it.</p></div>
  </div>
</div>
