<h2>What the job covers</h2>

<div class="deflist">
  <div class="deflist__row">
    <p class="deflist__term">Building the offer</p>
    <div class="deflist__desc"><p>What we sell, how it is positioned and priced, how it gets
    delivered, and the internal case needed to fund it.</p></div>
  </div>
  <div class="deflist__row">
    <p class="deflist__term">Carrying the P&amp;L</p>
    <div class="deflist__desc"><p>Revenue and operating performance together. Deciding which
    opportunities to turn down is part of that.</p></div>
  </div>
  <div class="deflist__row">
    <p class="deflist__term">Selling in the region</p>
    <div class="deflist__desc"><p>Turning a broad transformation proposition into something a
    particular market, in a particular country, will actually buy.</p></div>
  </div>
  <div class="deflist__row">
    <p class="deflist__term">Large-account cycles</p>
    <div class="deflist__desc"><p>Long processes where the decision is shared between HR, IT,
    finance and the executive committee.</p></div>
  </div>
  <div class="deflist__row">
    <p class="deflist__term">Working with executives</p>
    <div class="deflist__desc"><p>Staying with C-level sponsors through the life of a
    programme, including after the contract is signed.</p></div>
  </div>
  <div class="deflist__row">
    <p class="deflist__term">Sales, advisory and delivery</p>
    <div class="deflist__desc"><p>Keeping the commercial promise connected to what the team can
    build and run. This is where credibility is won or lost.</p></div>
  </div>
  <div class="deflist__row">
    <p class="deflist__term">Leading teams</p>
    <div class="deflist__desc"><p>Consultants, architects and managers across several countries,
    languages and working cultures.</p></div>
  </div>
</div>

<h2>Before Sopra HR</h2>

<div class="measure">
  <p>I did not start running things with a corporate mandate. In 2013 I left to found Créative
  RH Consulting, and from 2015 to 2017 I was Partner and General Manager for Morocco at Advyteam
  Group, where I built the consulting activity and set up Advyteam Academy.</p>
  <p>Before all of that, as Head of HR for an airline group of more than 6,000 employees, I was
  on the client side of the conversations I now lead. That still helps me hear what a CHRO is
  actually worried about.</p>
</div>
