  useful thing about the way I work.</p>

<h2>Why I keep them together</h2>

<div class="measure">
  <p>Because the problems arrive together. A CHRO asks me about a target operating model and
  we end up talking about who owns the employee record. A CIO asks about integration and the
  real question turns out to be which HR decisions get made locally.</p>
  <p>How I go about it is described in
