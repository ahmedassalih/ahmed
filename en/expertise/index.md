<div class="measure">
  <p>Transformation programmes are rarely undone by technology alone. The risk tends to
  concentrate in the gaps: between what the executive committee decided and what the target
  model actually requires, between the system that was selected and the data it will run on,
  between the go-live date and the day people change how they work.</p>
  <p>These three pillars are the places I work. They are deliberately connected.</p>
</div>

<h2>How the three connect</h2>

<div class="measure">
  <p>A target operating model that ignores the data model is a slide. An architecture that
  ignores the business case does not survive its first budget review. A programme that ignores
  adoption produces a system nobody uses and a benefits case nobody can defend.</p>
  <p>The working sequence — diagnose, architect, make it live — is described in
  <a href="/en/ideas/logician-engineer-method/">the Logician-Engineer Method</a>.</p>
</div>
