<h2>Short biography</h2>

<div class="panel">
  <p>Ahmed Assalih is an Executive HR Transformation &amp; AI Strategist with 25 years of
  experience across MEA and Europe. An engineer by training, he leads HR Transformation for
  Sopra HR Software in the MEA region, where he launched and developed the HR Transformation

<h2>Long biography</h2>

<div class="panel">
  <p>Ahmed Assalih is an Executive HR Transformation &amp; AI Strategist who works at the
  intersection of business leadership, human resources and technology. He has 25 years of
  professional experience across MEA and Europe, beginning in 2001.</p>
  <p>An engineering graduate of ENSEEIHT in Toulouse, he started his career at Royal Air Maroc,
  where he led the deployment of HR Access for the group and around ten subsidiaries, managing a
  mixed team of developers and HR experts. He then moved into Talent and Recruiting leadership
  before becoming Head of the HR Department of the group, with responsibility for an
  organisation of more than 6,000 employees.</p>
  <p>In 2013 he founded Créative RH Consulting, and from 2015 to 2017 was Partner and General
  Manager for Morocco at Advyteam Group, where he built the consulting activity and founded
  Advyteam Academy. Since 2017 he has led HR Transformation for Sopra HR Software in the MEA
  region, launching and developing the HR Transformation Business Unit across business
  development, advisory and delivery, with business and P&amp;L leadership, C-level
  relationships and regional transformation programmes, including a unified Talent Management
  transformation spanning more than 50 entities in 22 countries.</p>
  <p>He holds a post-graduate degree (DESS) in Airline Management from Aix-Marseille, was twice
  selected to represent Morocco at the International Mathematical Olympiads, and represented
  Morocco and Africa at the World Sudoku Championship in 2009.</p>
  <p>He is a guest professor on several HR Master's programmes, a conference speaker and a
  mentor. His 2026 book, <em>The Death of Skills</em>, argues that AI exposes the limits of
  skills-based talent models and requires organisations to rethink potential, adaptability,
  learning velocity and talent trajectories.</p>
</div>

<h2>Speaker positioning</h2>

<div class="measure">
  <p>Ahmed draws on programmes he has led, systems he has built and an HR function he has
  run. Sessions are delivered in French or English.</p>
  <p>Core themes: AI and the future of work; <em>The Death of Skills</em>; Fortress, Front Line,
  Laboratory; Time-to-Skill; Pay-for-Agility; rethinking skills architectures; HR
  transformation; HRIS in the AI era; responsible AI for HR.</p>
  <p>Formats: keynote (45 to 90 min), masterclass (approx. 3 hours), workshop (half day), round
  table (60 to 90 min). Full detail on the <a href="/en/speaking/">Speaking &amp; Teaching</a>
  page.</p>
</div>
